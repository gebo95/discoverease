# Trips V1

The Trips tab shows the active trip, trips available to switch into, and the
existing memory examples. Create a trip by choosing one of the curated
destinations; naming it and choosing a date range are optional. A trip without
dates starts with one flexible day. A dated trip receives one itinerary day per
calendar date.

The active trip can be edited from the Trips card or its dashboard. Changing a
date range resizes the day list and keeps planned experiences; if days are
removed, their experiences are retained on the final remaining day. Planned
experiences can be removed from the itinerary after confirmation.

Creation, switching, editing, and itinerary changes are in-memory only in V1.
Firebase will provide account-backed persistence later. Flight data, bookings,
maps, and location services are not part of this flow.
